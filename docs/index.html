01 / THE VISION

Beyond a number.
Into a feeling.

Sleep has texture. Some nights unfold like open water. Others arrive in fragments. We’re exploring a visual language that makes those differences felt.

The ambition is a private, automated series of cinematic reflections, shaped by recorded sleep patterns. A consistent creative identity; a different atmosphere each day.

THE CONCEPT INPUTS

All values are invented. Scene presets illustrate a creative direction; they do not classify your sleep. No Apple Sleep Score is used or recreated.

A missing signal stays unknown. A difficult night can feel unsettled without becoming frightening. Visual intensity is an artistic choice, never a diagnosis.

PRIVATE BY INTENTION

Share the vision.
Protect the person.

This showcase contains synthetic interactive examples, original web artwork and one prototype film based on real sleep data, shared with the data owner’s explicit permission. It has no health-data upload, analytics, tracking cookies or third-party media embeds. Underlying health records, other private films and internal production systems stay out of the public repository.

DreamPulse is an experimental art and design project, not a medical device or diagnostic service. GitHub hosts this website and may process standard request information under its own privacy policy.
